Jenish Tank

Ph.D. student in Biological Science, IISER Bhopal — Immuno-microbiology & Nano-biotechnology

I'm a Ph.D. student at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Bhopal, working at the intersection of immuno-microbiology and nano-biotechnology. My background spans microbiology, life sciences, and hands-on lab work — from biologically synthesizing nanoparticles to studying host-pathogen interactions and protein structure.

Research interests

Immuno-microbiology

Research on host–microbe interactions and immune responses during infection, with a focus on the regulatory roles of lncRNAs and circRNAs in infectious diseases. The work further explores lncRNA- and circRNA-derived micropeptides and their emerging roles in host immunity and pathogen–host interactions.

Nano-biotechnology

Green and chemical synthesis of diverse nanoparticles for biomedical applications, with a focus on their development as efficient drug delivery systems. The research explores nanoparticle characterization, drug loading, controlled release, cellular uptake, and therapeutic efficacy.

Translational Research & Targeted Delivery

Advancing strategies for targeted therapeutic delivery and translating laboratory findings into clinically relevant applications.
